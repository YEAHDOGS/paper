03 — QUESTIONS
Is this real money?+

No. PAPER is a simulator. No real money moves, nothing here is financial advice, and nothing is a real order.

Do I need an account?+

No login, no signup. Open the page and trade. Positions live in your device's localStorage.

Where does my data go?+

Nowhere. There is no backend. Export a JSON backup from the Rules tab before switching devices.

Which feeds are live?+

Crypto: CoinGecko, keyless, 60s refresh. Stocks: your free Finnhub or Twelve Data key. Futures, options, prediction: manual.

Can I trade with code?+

Yes. The app exposes a window.Paper API for paper-only bots. Close the tab and they stop.
